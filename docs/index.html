How it works

Four steps, a couple of seconds.

  1. 01

    Hear

    Records until you stop talking and turns it into text.

    Whisper · on your PC
  2. 02

    Think

    Understands the request and picks the right tool.

    Ollama · or OpenAI
  3. 03

    Act

    Does it natively on Windows — and asks first before looking at your screen.

    Rust · Win32
  4. 04

    Speak

    Answers in a natural voice and listens again if it asked you something.

    Piper · on your PC

Privacy

Byte runs on your PC by default. It asks before it looks at your screen. It forgets a conversation after a few quiet minutes. It only remembers what you ask it to. And it never says it did something it didn't.

Setup

Ready in about ten minutes.

Pick a brain

Install Ollama and pull any model — these are Byte's suggestions. Or skip this and use an OpenAI key.

> ollama pull granite4.2:3b
> ollama pull minicpm-v4.6:latest

Add ears and a voice

Byte supports Whisper for hearing and Piper for speaking. Put them with a Whisper model and a Piper voice in:

# %APPDATA%\Byte\models
whisper-cli.exe
ggml-tiny.en.bin
piper.exe
en_US-lessac-high.onnx
en_US-lessac-high.onnx.json

Install Byte

Run the .msi from GitHub Releases, or build it with Rust:

> git clone https://github.com/Snaehath/Byte.git
> cd Byte/src-tauri
> cargo tauri build

Say hello

Choose Local or Cloud on the welcome screen. Then say “Hey Byte”, click the orb, or press Ctrl + B. Byte waits in the system tray when you hide it.

FAQ

Good questions.

Is Byte free?

Yes. Byte is free, and local mode costs nothing to run. In cloud mode, OpenAI bills your own account for the requests you make.

Does it work offline?

Yes, in local mode. Hearing, thinking and speaking all happen on your PC. Only web search, YouTube and cloud mode need a connection.

Do I need a powerful GPU?

No. Small Ollama models run on most modern laptops; a GPU just makes them faster. On machines with little video memory, cloud mode is the quickest option.

What does cloud mode send to OpenAI?

The text of your request, plus a screenshot only when you ask Byte to look at your screen and say yes. Your voice recording never leaves your PC — it's transcribed locally first.

Can I turn off “Hey Byte”?

Yes — in Settings or from the tray menu. You can still click the orb or press Ctrl + B.

Something broke. Where do I report it?

Open an issue on GitHub and attach Byte's log from the tray menu → “Open log folder”.
